DriveTwin demonstration notices

This browser demonstration presents recorded SUMO simulation outcomes. It does not control a real vehicle, operate a live station, or run a new energy forecast.

  • Original DriveTwin code and the simulated vehicle scenario: MIT license.
  • Road geometry, road names, and the map backdrop: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL-1.0. Regional extract supplied by BBBike. A map snapshot may be incomplete or out of date.
  • Route elevation derived from Natural Resources Canada high-resolution elevation products under the Open Government Licence – Canada. No assumption of a flat route is introduced by this viewer.
  • Battery readings and charging availability are simulated. Charging bays describe the simulation scenario and are not a real-world availability service.
  • React and React DOM: MIT. Leaflet: BSD-2-Clause. Inter font: SIL Open Font License 1.1.
