Help with Letterboxd imports

In a desktop browser, export your data from your Letterboxd account settings. Choose the original ZIP in CineTaste, up to 25 MiB. Letterboxd’s export guide (opens in a new tab)

The import won’t open
Download a fresh copy and select the ZIP without unpacking it. Try the demo to check whether CineTaste can load its catalog. If the demo also fails, reload the page in a current desktop browser and try again.
Some films are missing
Open Watchlist coverage to see how many unseen films could be ranked. CineTaste needs a unique title-and-year match and genre information. Films marked watched are excluded from picks.
The filters returned nothing
Clear filters to see which films are eligible, then try a different genre or year range. If the export contains no unseen watchlist films, add films on Letterboxd and export it again, or try the demo.
Privacy

CineTaste needs no account. It reads the ZIP in your browser and uses your watchlist, watched history, diary entries, ratings, and film likes to rank regular picks on your device.

When you ask AI chat for unseen picks, Cloudflare receives your request, relevant earlier exchanges about unseen picks, and a short list of eligible catalog titles. Rewatch requests, your ZIP, ratings, likes, and viewing history stay in the browser. Cloudflare privacy policy

The app keeps your session in memory. Choose Clear session to discard the import and its picks. Reloading or closing the page also ends the session.

The site serves the app and its public film catalog. CineTaste includes no analytics. The host can still see ordinary requests for those public files. The sample uses invented viewing activity.

Film data and credits

Catalog 2026-11-25.1 contains 100,832 films. CineTaste uses omdb.org as its primary source and Wikidata for selected genre and title gaps. Catalog coverage varies by film.

Cast and director searches use omdb.org credits for films in its part of the catalog. CineTaste selects acting and directing credits and normalizes names for lookup.
