## Comparing approaches to equipment-failure classification

This academic project examined neural-network, decision-tree, and logical-analysis models for five industrial equipment-failure modes. My work focused on data preparation and comparison of the classification approaches.

## The workflow

Feature engineering and SMOTE were part of the preparation workflow. The model comparison considered different ways to use equipment data to distinguish failure modes.

The project extended my work on maintenance systems into data analysis: here, the engineering task was preparing the inputs and comparing classification methods for equipment-failure data.
